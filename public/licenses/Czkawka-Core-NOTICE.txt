AI Canvas uses Czkawka Core's complete-file BLAKE3 hash calculation for
ComfyUI model duplicate detection. Authorization, scan scope, file identity,
replacement confirmation, link transactions and recovery are implemented
by AI Canvas. No Czkawka graphical frontend is bundled.
